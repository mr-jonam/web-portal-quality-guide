What you can take away

  • Frame identity, business handovers, accessibility and performance questions together.
  • Recognise the limits of automated accessibility evidence.
  • Produce a safe first-contact brief about a portal journey.

Before you use this guide

Does this audit a portal or check WCAG conformance?

No. It asks orientation questions. Conformance needs an appropriately scoped evaluation and evidence.

Do I have to share a URL or customer information?

No. The mini tool asks only categorical questions and does not accept files or connect to targets.
