A focused first conversation

Bring the question your decision depends on.

A useful enquiry starts with a business outcome, the broad change category and one unresolved evidence question.

What to ask for

A discovery conversation to clarify the question, its scope and the information needed to judge a possible collaboration. Scope, availability and responsibilities are agreed before any professional engagement.

Prepare your request

Copy the draft below and edit it locally. If you used the mini tool, paste only the points you want to share. Preparing this text sends nothing.

Discuss your testing question on LinkedIn

Opens an external contact channel. Copy only the points you choose to share; this page does not send your draft or answers. A first conversation can clarify whether an assessment, testing engagement or collaboration would be useful.

Keep the first contact simple

No credentials, project histories, customer names, production records, instance URLs, screenshots, exports or internal documents are required. Do not use public GitHub issues to request a private assessment.
