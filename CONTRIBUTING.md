# Editorial feedback

External contributions are not currently accepted. Public issues are disabled; do not rely on the Issues tab for support.

For a correction, use the [maintainer's approved LinkedIn contact](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manoj-rollo/) and include only:

- repository and page name;
- the unclear sentence or broken public link;
- what you expected to read and the correction you suggest;
- an authoritative public reference, when relevant.

A small wording correction or a broken reference is a useful first report. Do not send implementation code, datasets, client examples, environment details, credentials or assessment documents. An editorial report is separate from a professional service enquiry.

The maintainer reviews suggestions under the repository's existing [licence](LICENSE). Sending feedback grants no new public reuse rights and does not promise a response or acceptance. A future contribution model would require a separate rights decision.
